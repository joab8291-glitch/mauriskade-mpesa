Terms and Conditions

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • You sign in with the login management gave you, and you buy water credit for your own meter only.
  • You pay with M-Pesa. When payment is confirmed, your token appears in the portal and you key it into the meter.
  • If you are charged but no token appears, do not pay again. Contact management with your M-Pesa receipt.
  • Used credit cannot be refunded.

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you use the water credit portal run by Mauriskade Apartments ("we", "us"). By signing in or making a payment, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the portal. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal data.

2. Who can use the portal

The portal is for adult residents and occupants who have been given a login by management. Each login belongs to one meter. You may only buy credit for the meter on your own account.

3. Your account

  • Management gives you a first password. You must replace it with your own the first time you sign in.
  • Keep your login private. You are responsible for what happens under your account, including payments made with it.
  • If you think someone else knows your password, tell management straight away so they can reset it.
  • After five wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
  • We may switch off a login, for example if you move out, if it is misused, or if management needs to protect the system.

4. Buying water credit

  1. You enter an amount and the Safaricom number you will pay from.
  2. You receive an M-Pesa prompt on that phone and approve it with your PIN.
  3. Once M-Pesa confirms the payment, the portal asks the meter platform for your token and shows it to you.
  4. You enter the 20-digit token on the meter keypad to add the credit.

Price and amounts

  • Water is sold at the rate set on the meter platform by management, currently a fixed price per cubic metre (m³). The portal shows an estimate before you pay. The volume actually credited is the one recorded by the meter platform.
  • Credit is sold in steps of 0.01 m³ and rounded down. A small remainder smaller than the price of 0.01 m³ is not turned into credit.
  • The smallest and largest amount you can pay at once are shown on the payment screen.
  • We do not add a portal fee to your payment. Any charges from Safaricom or your mobile provider are set by them.
  • Management may change the water rate. The new rate applies to payments made after the change.

5. Payments and tokens

  • Pay only from an M-Pesa number you are entitled to use.
  • Tokens usually appear within seconds. Sometimes they take longer because of the network, M-Pesa or the meter platform. If yours is delayed, keep the page open or come back to it. You do not need to pay again.
  • A token works only for the meter it was issued for. Treat it like cash. Anyone who has it can use it, and we cannot cancel a token once you have entered it.
  • Your past tokens stay in your top-up history so you can look them up again.

6. Failed, delayed or disputed payments

  • If you cancel the M-Pesa prompt, or it times out, no money is taken.
  • If money was taken but no token appears, do not pay again. Contact the Mauriskade Apartments management office and give your M-Pesa receipt number.
  • We will check the payment against the meter platform's records. Where a token has not been issued, management will either issue the correct token or arrange a refund to your M-Pesa number.
  • Credit that has already been issued and entered on the meter cannot be refunded.

Misuse may lead to your login being switched off and, where the law is broken, a report to the authorities under laws such as the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018.

We work to keep it running, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. We may pause it for maintenance. If it is down when you need credit, contact management.
