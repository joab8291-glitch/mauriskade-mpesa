Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to sign you in, take your M-Pesa payment and give you your water token.
  • We never see your M-Pesa PIN. You enter it on your own phone.
  • We do not sell your data, send marketing, or track you across other websites.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Mauriskade Apartments ("we", "us") runs this portal so residents can buy prepaid water credit for their meter. Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya, we are the data controller for the personal data described here.

The portal is built and maintained for us by Webazi Digital Solutions, Thika, Kenya. Webazi Digital Solutions handles your data only on our instructions, as our data processor.

Questions about your data go to the Mauriskade Apartments management office.

The first time you pay, we save the phone number you used and offer it again next time so you do not have to retype it.

3. How we use your data, and why

  • To provide the service. Signing you in, sending the M-Pesa prompt, requesting your token from the meter platform and showing your history. This is needed to deliver what you asked for.
  • To keep the portal secure. Limiting repeated sign-in attempts, locking an account for 15 minutes after five wrong passwords, and blocking misuse.
  • To resolve payment problems. If you are charged but no token appears, we compare our records with M-Pesa and the meter platform to fix it.
  • To meet legal and accounting duties. Keeping proper financial records.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and no decision about you is made by automated profiling.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only where the service needs it:

  • Safaricom PLC (M-Pesa). Receives your phone number, the amount and your meter number as the payment reference, so it can send the prompt and take payment.
  • The meter platform provider (the Hydro IoT water-meter platform). Receives your meter number and the amount paid so it can generate your token. It also holds the meter records and recharge history.
  • Our hosting providers. Render runs our backend and database. Vercel serves the website pages.
  • Google Fonts. Your browser downloads the portal's typefaces from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request.
  • Management and the developer. Authorised management staff and Webazi Digital Solutions can see account and payment records to run and support the portal.
  • Authorities. Only where the law requires it, for example a court order.

5. Data stored outside Kenya

Our hosting providers may keep data on servers outside Kenya. Where that happens, we rely on providers that apply security safeguards, as the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires for transfers abroad.

6. How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions expire after 7 days, or when you sign out, and expired sessions are deleted.
  • Payment records are kept for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute purposes, which is generally at least five years.
  • Account details are kept while you are a resident using the meter. When you move out, we disable your login and, on request, remove personal details we no longer need.

7. How we protect it

  • Your connection to this portal is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored using a one-way scrambling method. Staff cannot see your password, only reset it.
  • Your sign-in cookie cannot be read by scripts on the page.
  • You can only see top-ups and tokens for your own meter.
  • Access to the admin area needs a private key held by management.

No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your password private, treat your token like cash, and sign out on shared phones. If you think someone else has your password, tell management so they can reset it.

8. Cookies

We use one cookie, hp_session. It keeps you signed in and is deleted when you sign out or after 7 days. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete,
  • ask us to delete data we no longer need, apart from records the law requires us to keep,
  • object to or ask us to limit how we use your data, and
  • receive your data in a common format.

To use any of these rights, contact the Mauriskade Apartments management office. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we will reply within the time the law allows.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke.

10. Children

This portal is for adult residents. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell residents through the portal or management.
